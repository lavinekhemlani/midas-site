FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Midas replace my accountant?

It replaces most of the manual work. On the Controller and Fractional CFO plans our team does the bookkeeping, reconciliations and tax prep for you. If you already have an accountant or auditor, Midas gives them clean books to work from.

Do I have to switch accounting software?

No. Midas sits on top of Xero, QuickBooks, Zoho Books or NetSuite and reads your bank data. You keep what you use today.

Does it handle UAE corporate tax and VAT?

Midas tracks taxable profit, VAT positions and filing deadlines all year and prepares the numbers for your returns. Filings are reviewed by qualified professionals before submission.

How fast can we get started?

Most companies connect their systems in a day. Cleaning up past months depends on the state of your books, usually one to three weeks.

Is my data safe?

Connections are read-only, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you control who on your team can see what.

Can Midas help us raise capital?

Yes. On the Fractional CFO plan our team prepares the model and materials and introduces you to debt and equity investors.
