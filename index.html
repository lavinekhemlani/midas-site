Two structures, both simple

Rent-to-own — Ijara wa Iqtina
The bank owns the car and you rent it for a fixed monthly amount. With each payment, ownership passes to you step by step. At the end of the term the car is yours outright.
Cost-plus sale — Murabaha
The bank buys the car at the showroom price and sells it to you at a disclosed, fixed margin, paid in equal monthly instalments. You own the car from day one; the bank holds a lien until you finish paying.

Privacy notice

What we do with your information.

Plain language, as the Nigeria Data Protection Act (2023) intends. Questions: lavine@zenith-grp.co.

What we collect. What you type here: your name, phone, email, employment and income details, the car and structure you chose, and — with your consent — a verified income summary from a licensed open-banking provider. We never ask for a banking password, card PIN or OTP.
Why. One purpose: to arrange vehicle financing for you. Your file goes to the licensed banks on the panel so they can price an offer, and to licensed credit bureaus only after you tick that consent.
Who sees it. The banks pricing your application, the showroom completing your purchase, and our staff working on your file. We do not sell your information, ever, to anyone.
Where money moves. Your down payment is paid directly into the financing bank's account. Midas never holds your money.
How long we keep it. Active applications: while they are live. Funded loans: as long as the law requires financial records to be kept. Unsubmitted drafts stay only in your own browser and never reach us.
Your rights. Ask what we hold, correct it, or have it deleted where the law allows. Email us; a person answers.
Terms

The deal, plainly.

Midas is not a lender. We arrange financing between you, licensed banks, and the showroom. Every loan decision is the bank's own, made in the bank's own systems. Loan contracts are between you and the bank.
Prices you see before an offer letter are indicative. Monthly figures on this site are estimates from panel policy. Your binding terms arrive on the bank's offer letter, and only there.
The PFI fixes the car and price for 7 days. It is not a loan approval.
Your down payment goes only to the bank account named on the offer letter. Anyone asking you to pay a deposit anywhere else — including anyone claiming to be Midas — is defrauding you. Report it to us immediately.
Midas is paid by its partners, not by you. There is no customer fee for applying.
Vehicles are sold by the showroom with the manufacturer's warranty; after-sales service is the showroom's obligation to you.
