Read this before using the totals

  • The consolidation does not tie out — a ₦27.4bn gap. Assets less liabilities is ₦62.9bn, while equity plus retained result is ₦90.3bn. Expected at this stage, and it is the number that must reach zero before anyone signs anything.
  • Mixed period ends. Flexi is June, Premium and BSW August, Agro September. These are not the same balance-sheet date.
  • No intercompany eliminations. Due-to and due-from related-party balances are still in the numbers; Flexi alone carries ₦82m due from related parties.
  • Divisional reports use opposite sign conventions for liabilities and equity. Normalised by hand here, which is exactly the manual step the system is meant to remove.

What the numbers say

  • The group is Premium Aquaculture. Fish is 88% of assets, 86% of revenue and 94% of profit. The other three are, financially, a rounding error against it.
  • One entity is loss-making: Polypack BSW, ₦1.48bn negative year to date and deepening ₦200–350m a month.
  • Group margin is 12.0% — but that is Premium's margin wearing a group hat.
  • Working capital is concentrated too: ₦136.5bn of ₦135.7bn group NWC sits in Premium; every other entity is near zero or negative.

To make this audit-grade

  • Align the period ends — one common close date across all four.
  • Get the intercompany matrix so due-to and due-from can be eliminated pair by pair.
  • Add the missing entities. Four of roughly 69 are connected; auto, rice and the UAE trading companies are absent, and auto is the largest business by volume.
  • Bring in the ledgers — the 523,410 transactions already sitting in the Easy Reports SQL sync would replace hand-normalised summaries with real detail.
