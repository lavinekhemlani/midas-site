Capital efficiency frontier

Each dashed ray is a constant return per dollar-year. A company above a ray beats that return; below it, falls short. Bubble area is capital employed (receivables + inventory − payables). Because the contours are rays from the origin, margin and speed trade off directly — which is why a lower-margin, faster business can sit above a higher-margin, slower one.
