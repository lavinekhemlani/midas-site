Settled under FSA2513296-26CRE001 / LCD-5002228442 instead of its own FSA2513383-26CRE001 / LCD-5002228780. The two cancel exactly, so the obligation is discharged — but read literally the workbook shows this drawing 153,948 unpaid and the other over-settled by the same amount.

A settlement booked against the wrong reference leaves one drawing looking overdue and another over-settled by an identical amount, for the same supplier. Matched and reported here rather than silently netted. Read at face value the book would show $153,948 past due that is not actually owed.
