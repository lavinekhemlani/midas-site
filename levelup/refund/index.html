Refund policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 (draft)

We want you to enrol without risk. These are our three promises.

1. Full refund up to the halfway point

You can cancel for any reason and get a full refund up to the halfway point of your course:

  • Flagship tracks (8 sessions): until the end of session 4.
  • AI at Work short course (4 sessions): until the end of session 2.

Email lavine@zenith-grp.co or message us on WhatsApp. We refund to your original payment method within 10 working days, in the currency you paid.

2. Free repeat seat

If you complete a flagship track and want another run at it, you can rejoin a future cohort of the same track once at no extra cost, space permitting.

3. Outcome guarantee (flagship tracks)

  • AI for Automation: ship one real automation live in your job by session 8, or your money back.
  • AI for Entrepreneurs: leave with a working AI-powered offer and your first outreach sent, or your money back.

To claim, you need to have attended or watched the replay of all 8 sessions and submitted the weekly build tasks. Claim within 14 days of the final session.

Before the course starts

If you cancel before the first session, you get a full refund. If we cancel or move a cohort, you can choose a full refund or a seat in the next cohort.

Reserved, unpaid seats

No payment is taken for a "reserve now, pay later" request until you complete payment through your secure link.
