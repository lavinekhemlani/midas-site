Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 November 2026 (draft)

This notice explains how Zenith Group ("LevelUp", "we") collects and uses your personal data. We follow the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of Hong Kong (PDPO). Where it applies to you, we also follow the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we collect

  • Details you give us: name, email, WhatsApp number, course, region and currency.
  • Payment details. These are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Course records: attendance, projects you submit and your certificate status.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends when you visit the site.

Why we use it

  • To process your application, reservation, payment and enrolment.
  • To run the course and contact you about sessions, payment links and schedule changes.
  • To send course updates, if you agreed. You can opt out at any time.

Under GDPR, our legal bases are performing our contract with you, your consent (for marketing) and our legitimate interest in running and improving our courses.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run LevelUp: Stripe (payments), FormSubmit (form delivery), our email provider, WhatsApp (if you message us) and Skool (course community). Some of these process data outside Hong Kong, including in the United States. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for direct marketing by third parties.

How long we keep it

We keep enrolment and payment records for as long as the law requires, normally 7 years for accounting records. Enquiries that do not lead to enrolment are deleted after 24 months.

Your rights

Under the PDPO, you can ask for access to your personal data and ask us to correct it. Under GDPR, you can also ask us to delete or restrict it, object to its use, ask for a portable copy, and withdraw consent. You may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (Hong Kong) or to your local data protection authority.

Contact

Data access and correction requests: lavine@zenith-grp.co. We aim to respond within 40 days, as the PDPO requires.

LevelUp is operated by Zenith Group.
