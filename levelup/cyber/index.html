Bootcamp curriculum

twelve weeks, four blocks.

Every week ends with a scored lab. You leave with a portfolio of real findings, not a certificate of attendance.

WEEKS 1–3

Foundations

Networks, Linux, cloud basics, how attacks work, and the threat landscape in Asia.

WEEKS 4–6

Blue team

SOC operations, logs and SIEM, incident response, and a simulated breach you have to contain.

WEEKS 7–9

Red team and AI

Ethical hacking, web app testing, then AI red-teaming: prompt injection, jailbreaks and agent abuse.

WEEKS 10–12

Capstone and hiring

Test a real system end to end, write the report, present findings, then meet hiring partners.
