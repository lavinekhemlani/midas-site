What you'll learn

four weeks, eight live sessions.

Week 1

Find the work worth doing

Map where AI pays off in your business or your job, and pick the one thing you will ship.

Week 2

Build the engine

Prompting that holds up, then your first real workflow or offer prototype running end to end.

Week 3

Make it reliable

Agents, data and documents, plus the checks that stop AI output from embarrassing you.

Week 4

Ship it live

Put it in front of real users or clients, measure it, and present it to the cohort.

Is this for you?

honest fit check.

This is for you if

  • You use ChatGPT or Claude but know you are only scratching the surface
  • You want something shipped in four weeks, not a certificate for watching videos
  • You run a business, a team or a side project and want AI doing real work in it
  • You can give it about 4 to 5 hours a week

This is not for you if

  • You want to become a machine learning engineer or train models from scratch
  • You are looking for a free or subsidised course
  • You want passive income or a get-rich-quick plan
  • You want self-paced videos with no live sessions
  • You can't make most of the live sessions or catch up on replays

Questions

before you enrol.

Is this actually live, or am I watching recordings?

Every core session runs live with a real instructor, in your region's scheduled time. Replays exist so you never lose a session you miss.

When do the January 2027 cohorts start?

Hong Kong starts Saturday 9 January 2027. US and Southeast Asia start Tuesday 12 January 2027. The AI at Work short course in Hong Kong starts Wednesday 13 January 2027.

Where is the Hong Kong venue?

Central, Hong Kong. The exact venue is confirmed when you enrol.

What's the difference between the short course and a flagship track?

AI at Work is four evenings in person in Hong Kong, focused on using AI in your current job. The flagship tracks are eight live sessions over four weeks and end with a shipped project. If you upgrade within 60 days, the short course fee is credited.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Join a free 90-minute live workshop above, or register and we send you the replay.

What's the refund policy?

Full refund up to the halfway point of your course, no questions asked. After that, each flagship track has an outcome guarantee: do the work and don't ship the stated deliverable by session 8, and you get your money back. See the refund policy.

What if I want to take it again?

You can rejoin a future cohort of the same track for free, space permitting.

Why pay now for a January cohort?

The early-bird price of US$897 ends on Sunday 29 November at 23:59 Hong Kong time. After that it is US$1,200. Your refund rights are the same either way: full refund up to the halfway point.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Choose three monthly payments, or a US$97 deposit that holds the early-bird price with the balance due before the cohort starts. Pay in full is the default.

Do I need paid tools?

You can do most of the course on free tiers. Some builds go further with a paid Claude or ChatGPT plan (about US$20 a month) or a paid automation plan. We tell you before each session what you need, and never require a tool we earn commission on.

There are free AI courses online. Why pay for this?

Free courses teach you what the tools do. Most people never finish them and never ship anything. Here you build one real thing for your own job or business, live, with an instructor and a small group checking your work every week.

Can I pay by FPS, PayMe or bank transfer?

Yes. Choose "Reserve now, pay later" at checkout and we send you FPS or bank transfer details for Hong Kong, or a secure card link anywhere else. Your seat is held for 7 days while you pay.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Use the email template above, and we can issue an invoice addressed to your company.

Which currency will I pay in?

You choose. We accept USD, HKD, SGD, AED, GBP, CAD and PHP. The short course is priced in HKD. Payment is handled securely by Stripe.

Is this subsidised or free through a government programme?

No. LevelUp is paid, independently run and founder-led. That is how we keep cohorts small and live.

I'm not technical. Can I still do this?

Yes. Both tracks are built for operators and entrepreneurs, not engineers. No coding background required.

How many seats are there, really?

24 per track, per region, per cohort. Once a cohort is full, you're offered the next one.
