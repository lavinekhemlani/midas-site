The Augusta Rule for Content Creators (Section 280A)

Midas — the financial team behind the creators · Reviewed for educational accuracy

The Augusta rule (IRC §280A(g)) lets you rent your home to your own business for up to 14 days a year — and the rental income is tax-free to you while being deductible to the business.

Why it fits creators unusually well

Creators already use their homes for business constantly: shoot days, planning sessions, brand-collab days, team summits. Documented properly, those are legitimate rental days at market rates. Illustration: a documented $3,000/day venue rate × 10 days = $30,000 deductible to your company, tax-free to you.

The three requirements that make it survive scrutiny

  • Market-rate comps: a file showing what comparable venues/spaces rent for in your area — hotels, studios, event spaces.
  • Real business purpose: agendas, attendees and outcomes for each rental day.
  • 14 days maximum: day 15 makes all of it taxable rental income.

The catch

This strategy is documentation-or-nothing. Done casually, it's an audit flag; done properly, it's one of the cleanest deductions in the code.
