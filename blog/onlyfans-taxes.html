OnlyFans Taxes: What Subscription Creators Need to Know

Midas — the financial team behind the creators · Reviewed for educational accuracy

Every dollar of subscription, tip and PPV income is taxable — OnlyFans and similar platforms report to the IRS, withhold nothing, and the creator carries the full compliance burden.

The three realities

  • You're a business: self-employment tax applies on top of income tax; quarterly payments are expected.
  • Deductions are real: equipment, lighting, wardrobe used exclusively for content, home studio share, platform fees, contractors — documented, they materially cut the bill.
  • Privacy can be structured: an LLC/S-corp provides both tax efficiency and a professional layer between your legal identity and your business.

Why this niche overpays the most

High earnings, zero planning infrastructure, and advisors who don't understand the business. Subscription creators are among the highest-margin businesses in the creator economy — which makes the unplanned tax leak proportionally the largest, and the planning upside the biggest.
