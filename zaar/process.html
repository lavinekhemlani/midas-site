How we work

One producer, every cue.

From the first call to the last guest, one team owns the plan and one person answers for the night.

A typical timeline

Eight weeks, brief to show day.

Larger builds and exhibition stands start earlier. Private events can run in four weeks.

  1. 01

    Brief

    We meet, listen and visit the venue. You get one point of contact from the first call.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Moodboard, layout and the flow of the night, with options at two or three budgets.

  3. 03

    Design

    Stage, fabrication drawings, AV plan, artist line-up and a full run sheet.

  4. 04

    Build

    Fabrication in our workshop, AV prep and rehearsal before anything reaches the venue.

  5. 05

    Show day

    One producer on the floor calling every cue, with crew for AV, stage and artists.

  6. 06

    Wrap

    Strike on time, a short report with photos and video, and an invoice that matches the quote.
