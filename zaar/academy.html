Why an academy

Every room deserves a headliner.

A great night lives or dies on the person behind the decks. When we run six events on the same night, every one of them needs a DJ our clients would have booked by name.

The Academy is how we make sure of it. We find the best DJs and artists in the Middle East, train them to our standard, represent them, make music with them and put them on the best stages.
