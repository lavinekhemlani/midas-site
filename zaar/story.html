Our story

Production with an ear.

Most event companies start with the venue and add the music last. Zaar started at the decks, watching good nights fall flat because music, sound and light were run by three teams with three plans.

So he built one house to run them together. We don't just play the night. We run it.

What we believe in

Great standards. Period.

Our team

The people behind the night.

Founder & Creative Director

Abizaar Arsiwala

Abizaar Arsiwala, known as DJ Zaar, founded Z Productions after years behind the decks at Taj, St. Regis, Delano, The Ritz-Carlton Abu Dhabi, HIKARI and Opal Room DIFC.

He has played weddings and private nights in thirteen countries, from Cannes and Thailand to Phu Quoc and Sri Lanka. His sets move between Bollywood, Afro house and melodic techno, often with live tabla.

His releases include Sunrise Chahta Hai and Majboor, and his filmed live set from HIKARI Dubai is on YouTube.

Board Member

Lavine Hemlani

Lavine Hemlani is Managing Partner of Zenith Group.

He started his first company, Xccelerate, at 23 and built it over seven years into an AI and tech education platform that trained more than 20,000 people and placed graduates with over 500 companies. It was acquired by GetLinks in 2025.

Before that he worked in M&A at Lazard in New York, and in investing at Atlas Capital, Marwar Capital and Union Park Capital. He is a University of Chicago graduate, has spoken on CNN about AI and the future of work, and brings founders together through the Cap Table Network.

At Z Productions he leads corporate partnerships.
