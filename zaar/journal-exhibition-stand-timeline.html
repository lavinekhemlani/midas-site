Exhibition stands: a build timeline that works

From first sketch to show day, and where stands usually slip.

Eight to ten weeks out: brief and concept

Agree the footprint, the story you want visitors to leave with, and the budget band. A good concept answers what people will do on the stand, not just what it looks like.

Six weeks out: drawings and approvals

Technical drawings go to the organiser for approval. Rigging, power and fire rules vary by venue, so this step cannot be rushed.

Four weeks out: fabrication and content

Build starts in the workshop while LED content is produced to the exact screen sizes.

Build week: install, test, rehearse

Install on site, test every screen and cue, and walk the stand as a visitor before doors open.

Where stands slip

Late approvals, late content and changes after fabrication starts. Freezing the design at the drawings stage is the single best protection.
