AI manifesto

Faster planning. Human nights.

How we use AI, and where we never will.

  1. 01

    AI plans. People produce.

    We use AI to draft run sheets, test layouts and check budgets faster. Every decision on the night is made by a person on the floor.

  2. 02

    Your data stays yours.

    Guest lists, briefs and budgets are never used to train public AI models, and we sign an NDA on request.

  3. 03

    Honest images.

    Concept renders made with AI are labelled as concepts. Photos of our work are real photos of our work.

  4. 04

    Artists are paid and credited.

    We do not replace live performers with generated music or voices, and we credit every artist we book.

  5. 05

    Faster answers, same standard.

    AI helps us reply to a brief within one working day. It never lowers the standard of what we deliver.
