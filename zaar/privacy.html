Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Z Square Productions FZE LLC ("Z Productions", "we"), a company licensed in the United Arab Emirates under trade licence 2626617661888.

What we collect

This site does not use accounts, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not store the details you type into it.

When you use the enquiry form, your details (name, company, occasion, date, guests, venue and budget) are put into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. From then on, we receive what you chose to send, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, plan and quote your event, and keep records we are required to keep. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

We share event details only with the venues, crew and suppliers needed to deliver your event, and only as much as they need. We may share information where the law requires it.

Fonts and third parties

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. Links to Instagram, YouTube and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and event records for as long as we need them for the event and for our legal, tax and accounting obligations, then delete them.

Your rights

Under UAE data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Message us on WhatsApp at +971 52 872 5585 and we will respond.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
