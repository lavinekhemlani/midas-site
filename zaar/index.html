Questions

Before you ask.

What do you take care of?

Music, sound, lighting, LED, stage, effects and styling, planned together and run by one producer on the night.

Can you work with our venue, agency or planner?

Yes. We fit in alongside your venue and planners, or run the whole night ourselves.

Do you work outside Dubai?

Yes. We have played and produced events in thirteen countries, from Cannes to Phu Quoc.

How do we start?

Send the date, guest count and city. We come back with questions, a site visit, then one plan and one quote.
