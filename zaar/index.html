Questions

Before you ask.

What do you take care of?

Event design, AV, fabrication, styling and artists, planned together and run by one producer on the night.

Do you work with corporates directly?

Yes. We work directly with companies, funds, family offices and venues, or alongside your agency and planners.

Can you plan a full year of events?

Yes. Booking several events with one team, from a launch to a year-end, saves cost and time and keeps the standard the same.

Do you install AV permanently?

Yes. We design and install sound, light and LED for hotels, members clubs and lounges, and maintain it under an annual contract.

Do you work outside the UAE?

Yes. We have produced and performed at events in thirteen countries, from Cannes to Phu Quoc.

Who are we contracting with?

Z Square Productions FZE LLC, a licensed company in the United Arab Emirates.
