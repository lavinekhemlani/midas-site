About us

For more than fifteen years, Z² Productions has designed and produced events across the UAE and in thirteen countries, from bank galas and brand launches to destination weddings.

We bring design, AV, fabrication and artists under one team. That means one plan, one quote and one producer who answers for the whole night, so the host can stay with their guests.

Every event starts with the space and the people in it. We build the night around the venue and the character of the host, then hold every detail to the same standard, whether it is forty guests or four hundred.
