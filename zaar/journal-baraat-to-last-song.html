Baraat to last song: how a wedding day really runs

A wedding is four or five events in one day. The music has to carry all of them.

An Indian wedding in Dubai can start with a baraat in the hotel forecourt and end with a reception that runs past two in the morning. In between there may be a ceremony, a brunch, a sangeet and a change of venue.

The baraat

The dhol leads, the groom's side dances him in, and timing is everything: the music has to hold the energy until the bride's side is ready. Recent baraats at Delano and Taj taught us to always plan for the wait.

The quiet middle

Ceremonies and lunches need music that fills the room without competing with it. This is where most weddings feel flat.

The reception

Then the room turns. Bollywood, commercial and Afro house in one set, read live from the floor. The best receptions feel like the whole day was building to them, because it was.
