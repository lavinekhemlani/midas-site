Why the artist belongs in the production meeting

Most corporate events book the music last. The best ones start with it.

Walk into most event planning meetings in Dubai and you will find the venue, the caterer, the AV company and the decor team. The artist is usually a name on a spreadsheet, booked once the date is fixed and briefed in a WhatsApp message the week before.

That is backwards. The music decides when people stand up, when they sit down, when the speeches land and when the room finally lets go. It shapes the lighting plan, the stage position and where the bar should be.

What changes when the artist is in the room

When the person playing the night helps plan it, the run sheet gets simpler. Walk-ups are timed to the music. The lighting is cued to the drop, not to a guess. The speaker system is specified for the room, not for the last event the AV company did.

The practical version

Ask your artist to walk the venue. Share the run sheet early. Tell them who is in the room, not just how many. It costs nothing, and it is the difference between background music and a night people talk about.
