What an AV maintenance contract should cover

For hotels, venues and members' clubs that run events every week.

Scheduled servicing

Speakers, amplifiers, lighting and LED checked on a fixed calendar, with a short report after each visit.

Response times

A clear promise for how fast a technician arrives when something fails before a booked event.

Spares on hand

Critical spares held locally so a failed part does not cancel a night.

Upgrades and planning

An annual review of what to replace, upgrade or add, with prices agreed in advance.

One point of contact

The same team that maintains the system should know the room, so event nights run without surprises.
