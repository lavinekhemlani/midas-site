Benchmark

How "7 days to 2 minutes" is estimated.

The same month-end task, done once by a four-person finance team and once by Midas. This page sets out what was measured, how, and what the result does not include.

This is an estimate, not an audited benchmark. The figures below are our estimates for a typical multi-entity month-end. We measure the real numbers on your own data in a pilot.

Summary

The headline result.

Task: a monthly close through to the board pack for a multi-entity business. Midas time excludes your own review, which comes on top.

Method

How we estimate it.

  • The human side: typical time for each of the four roles to do its part of a month-end by hand, including the hand-offs between them.
  • The Midas side: one run, from connected systems to a drafted close and board pack.
  • What "done" means: reconciled books, variance analysis, a cash forecast and a board pack ready for review.

Caveats

What this does not include.

  • Your review and approval time. Midas prepares the work; people still sign it off.
  • First-time setup: connecting systems (about a day) and cleaning up history (usually one to three weeks).
  • Books that are already connected and cleaned up. Messy history takes longer the first time.
  • These are estimates. Your own figures will differ, which is why we measure them in a pilot.
