Ask, don't build

A dashboard shows you what. Midas tells you why.

In Power BI, a new question means a new report and a ticket for the developer. In Midas you just ask.

Why is cash down this month?
Cash is down AED 412K. 70% is the Al Noor receivable (AED 290K) now 45 days overdue. The rest is the USD supplier payment on the 12th. Want me to draft a reminder to Al Noor?
What's our VAT position for Q4 so far?
Net VAT payable is AED 86,400 across the three entities, due 28 Jan. Entity 2 has AED 9,100 of input VAT on invoices missing a TRN. I've listed them.
