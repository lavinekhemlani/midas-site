What we do with governments

four programmes. one platform.

🚌

Transport revenue

Digital fares across bus, rail and ferry. Every naira counted, settled daily and auditable, with live data on every route.

💼

Public servant credit

Fair salary loans through payroll, so public servants stop paying loan-app prices. No cost to the state.

🚗

Vehicle and housing schemes

Programmes that put cars, motorcycles and homes within reach of state and federal workers, repaid through payroll.

🏗️

Infrastructure finance

Revenue from new buses and transport can be ring-fenced to repay the finance that bought them, bringing in new fleets without new budget.

Why it matters

what a state gets.

No leakage

Cash disappears. Digital collections settle daily into accounts the state can see, with a full audit trail.

Happier workforce

Public servants get fair credit for school fees, rent and cars, without predatory lenders or shaming.

New assets, no new debt

Buses and vehicles financed against their own revenue, with private capital and international partners.

ezee is a technology company, not a bank. ezee loans are provided by Sage Grey Finance Limited, which is licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of Nigeria and integrated into the Nigerian Inter-Bank Settlement System (NIBSS). ezee pay, cards and savings are not yet live; when they launch they will be provided through licensed partners, named here. All loans are subject to eligibility, a credit check and affordability. Rates, amounts and screens shown on this site are examples. Targets and market figures are goals or third-party estimates, not ezee results.

Privacy. If you join the waitlist we use your name, WhatsApp number and the answers you choose only to contact you about ezee, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. We do not sell your data. To be removed, message us on WhatsApp.
