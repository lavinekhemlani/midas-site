Why we called it ezee

ezee

Say it out loud. It's "easy", the way Lagos says it. Because that's the whole point.

Money in Nigeria is hard. Failed transfers. Loan apps that shame you. Change for the bus. We named the company after the one thing everyone wants it to be.

e · easy

Pay with one tap. Borrow in minutes. Nothing to print, no branch to visit, no queue.

z · zero wahala

No hidden charges, no surprise fees and no calls to your family. Every naira shown before you agree.

ee · everyone, everywhere

From the danfo to the boardroom, Lagos to Kano. Built for all 200 million of us.

The mark

an e that moves forward.

Our mark is a lowercase e whose stroke turns into an arrow: money moving forward, one tap at a time. The yellow is the yellow of the Lagos danfo, the colour of a city that never stops moving.
