Why ezee

borrowing shouldn't feel like a fight.

Most loan apps lend without knowing you, then chase you to get paid back. ezee lends against your salary, so the money costs less and nobody has to chase anybody.

The usual loan app

  • ✕The priceSmall amounts, short deadlines and charges that pile up once you're late.
  • ✕Paying backYou chase the due date, or the due date chases you.
  • ✕If you're lateCalls and messages to the people in your phone.
  • ✕The fine printFees you only see after the money lands.

ezee credit

  • ✓The priceLower monthly cost, because your salary secures the loan. Bigger amounts, longer to repay.
  • ✓Paying backTaken from your salary on payday. Nothing to remember.
  • ✓If life happensWe talk to you, only you. We never contact anyone in your phone.
  • ✓The fine printOne monthly amount, every charge shown before you say yes.
