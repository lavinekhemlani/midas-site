Questions

straight answers.

When can I get a loan?

We're opening in Lagos first, starting with civil servants and staff of trusted employers. Join the waitlist and we'll message you on WhatsApp the moment your spot is ready.

Is ezee a bank?

No. ezee is a technology company. The loans are made by a lending partner licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria, and we'll show their name on this page before the first loan.

Who can apply?

Nigerians aged 18 and over with a regular salary paid into a Nigerian bank account. We start with government workers and staff of companies we partner with, then open to more people.

What do I need?

Your BVN, a recent payslip or bank statement, and a valid ID. No paperwork to print and no branch to visit.

How much does it cost?

You'll see one monthly amount and every charge before you accept. The calculator above uses 2.5% a month on a reducing balance as an example. Your real rate depends on your credit check.

Will you call my family or contacts?

No. Never. We only ever speak to you about your loan.

What if I change jobs?

Tell us early. We'll move your repayment to your new salary account or agree a plan that works for you.
