03 — Approach

Impact investing, not philanthropy.

Grants end when the money runs out. Good investments keep working. We hold impact capital to the same discipline as every other dollar on the balance sheet.

What we do

Invest for return and outcome

Every impact position has a business model, a cash flow and a measurable outcome.

  • Underwrite to a target return, then measure the outcome
  • Structure with downside protection: security, covenants, co-investment
  • Recycle profits into the next deal, so the capital compounds
  • Work with operators on the ground, not intermediaries
What we avoid

Capital that cannot return

We respect philanthropy. It is simply not what this office does.

  • Grants and donations without a business model
  • Outcomes we cannot measure or verify
  • Blind-pool commitments with no say in the asset
  • Leverage that turns a good asset into a forced seller
