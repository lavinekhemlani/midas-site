The science of the sachet

Formulated like
a medicine.

Most powders are a scoop of everything mixed together. Some ingredients fight each other, some dissolve away, some never get absorbed. Facet is built by pharmaceutical formulation scientists, phase by phase.

i.

The drink base

Water-soluble actives like creatine, L-theanine and the vitamin and mineral premix dissolve into a clean, lightly sparkling base that tastes like yuzu and white tea.

ii.

Protected microbeads

Fat-soluble actives that powders usually waste, like vitamins D3 and K2, are carried in tiny lipid microbeads that disperse when you stir and help your body absorb them.

iii.

Enteric granules

Glucoraphanin and its activating enzyme are coated separately so they survive your stomach and only meet in the gut, where they form sulforaphane. Kept apart until the moment they're needed.

iv.

Proven as a finished product

We don't just cite studies on single ingredients. We're planning human absorption data on the finished Facet sachet itself, the study most supplement brands never pay for.

Questions

Good questions.

Why not 90 ingredients like the big brands?

Because a long label isn't the same as a working formula. A single sachet only holds so much, so 90 ingredients means most are present in tiny amounts. We'd rather give you fewer ingredients at the doses used in research, and show you every number.

Why is there 5 g of creatine in a brain product?

Creatine isn't just for the gym. Your brain uses it to buffer energy, and research suggests the benefits for thinking show up most when you're tired, stressed or short on sleep. 5 g a day is the dose used in most of that research.

Is there caffeine?

No. Facet is designed to sit alongside your coffee, not replace it. L-theanine pairs especially well with your morning cup.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Some people notice calmer focus within days. Creatine and the defence ingredients build over weeks, which is exactly why we built the Index: so you can measure it rather than guess.

What does "pharmaceutical formulation" actually mean?

Instead of blending powders together, each ingredient is handled the way it needs to be: dissolved, protected in a lipid microbead, or coated so it survives your stomach. That's how drugs are formulated, and it's rare in supplements.

Is Facet safe for me?

Facet is a dietary supplement, not a medicine. If you're pregnant, nursing, under 18, taking medication or have a medical condition, speak to your doctor before starting.

When does Facet launch?

We're finishing the formula and first production run now. Founding members will be the first to know and the first to ship.
