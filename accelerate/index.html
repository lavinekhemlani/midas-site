Questions

before you apply.

Is this actually live, or am I watching recordings?

Every core session runs live with a real instructor, in your region's scheduled time. Replays exist so you never lose a session you miss — they're the backup, not the format.

What if I miss a live session?

It's recorded and yours to keep, with lifetime access. You can also bring questions on it to the optional Thursday AMA.

Is this subsidized or free through a government program?

No. Accelerate is paid, independently run, and founder-led — not a subsidized scheme. That's a deliberate choice: it's how we keep cohorts small and live.

What will I actually learn to use?

Automation track: n8n, Zapier, Make, and Claude's Cowork agent, building real workflows for your own job. Entrepreneurs track: building and launching a fixed-scope, fixed-price AI-powered offer using tools like Claude and ChatGPT plus no-code builders — not abstract "AI strategy."

I'm not technical. Can I still do this?

Yes — both tracks are built for operators and entrepreneurs, not engineers. No coding background required; mentors are there specifically to unblock you.

What's the refund policy?

Each track has its own outcome-based guarantee (see Pricing above) — ship the stated deliverable by session 8, or you get your money back.

What happens after the cohort ends?

You keep lifetime replay access, your certificate, and your seat in the private Skool community — the cohort ends, the access doesn't.

How many seats are there, really?

24 per track, per region, per cohort. That's a real operating cap, not a marketing number — once a cohort is full, you're offered the next one.
